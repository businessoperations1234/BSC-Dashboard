About this dashboard

Audience

Designed for the CEO's monthly review and quarterly board / investor / promoter distribution. Same source of truth, same numbers — read by management to act on, read by investors to evaluate.

What you're looking at

The structure mirrors the FY27 Board Overview deck. Three Strategic Goals at the top, then the live performance against the four BSC pillars — Financial, Customer, Internal Processes, Culture & People — followed by FY27 milestones and the success picture for the year.

How it stays current

The dashboard pulls live from the BSC master register on SharePoint. Anyone with read access to the file can refresh after signing in once with their organizational account. The AI Analyst at the bottom answers questions about the data currently in view — change the filters and the analyst's context changes with it.

Printing for board packs

Use your browser's Print → Save as PDF to export a board-ready document. Interactive controls are hidden in print; pillars and tables break cleanly across pages.
